Fig. 5 | Beyond about 2.6σ, junctions leave the harmonic cage together with their neighbourhood.
a, Two-state cage. Top: in the closed (phantom) cage the junction (vermillion) moves alone and stretches its own strands, with cage width σ. In the open cage, neighbouring junctions move with it (open circles mark their former positions), widening the cage to sσ. The open state is occupied a fraction p of the time. Bottom: effective potential U = −k_BT ln P̂ for PEGDA 20 kDa swollen for 2 h (grey), with the unit harmonic cage (dashed) and the two-state fit (vermillion). Dotted lines mark Δ* and U* = ½Δ*². Inset: restoring force F = dU/dΔ, from finite differences of the data (grey) and from the fit; it is linear inside the cage and saturates beyond Δ*.
b, Pooled, unscaled distributions of junction displacements δx about their mean positions for three precursor masses (swollen 2 h), with Gaussian fits (dashed; σ = 9.4, 11.7 and 12.9 nm). Pooling mixes junctions with different cage widths σ_i.
c, σ_i is a persistent property of each junction: cage widths from the first and second halves of each trajectory (PEGDA 5 kDa, n = 144 junctions). Inset: distribution of σ_i. Split-half values are estimated from the measured σ_i with the sampling error of 85 frames per half.
d–f, Rescaled distributions P̂(|Δ|), Δ = δx/σ_i, for precursor mass (d; filled symbols, 4-arm PEG-SH 20 kDa), swelling time (e; 20 kDa) and temperature (f; 20 kDa, swollen 1 h). Dashed: unit Gaussian (no free parameter). Lines: two-state fits, equation (1). Dotted: Δ*. The right axis of f gives U/k_BT.
g, Excess over the harmonic cage, P̂/G, for all 12 conditions (colours as in d–f). Every condition follows the cage (P̂/G = 1) up to |Δ| ≈ 2 and doubles at Δ* = 2.63 ± 0.11 (grey band; mean ± s.d., n = 12 conditions).
h, Parameters by condition: w, width of a Gaussian fit to the core (|Δ| < 2); Δ*; λ, decay length of an exponential fit to |Δ| ≥ 2.5; E_3σ, frequency of |Δ| > 3 relative to the harmonic cage, from the two-state fit. Error bars: s.e. of the fits. The 20 kDa, 2 h sample appears in both the precursor and the swelling series, measured on independent gels.
i, Fraction of junctions that a Shapiro–Wilk test (α = 0.05) flags as non-Gaussian, against trajectory length N. Simulated for Gaussian junctions (dashed) and for junctions that all share the pooled P̂ (line: median; band: range over the 12 conditions). Grey band: trajectory lengths in this study (estimated, 60–180 frames).
In b and d–g, about 10^6 displacements are pooled per condition. In d–g, x and y components and both signs of Δ are pooled after checking symmetry (Supplementary Fig. X).
